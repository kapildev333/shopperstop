## Phase 5 dispatch work package (planning session, 2026-10-16; PLAN.md D57)
Uber's dispatch, reduced to a dark store: supply state (online, offline), a tick that ranks the store's online riders by ETA to the store and offers the oldest ready order to the best one, a 30-second offer the rider accepts or declines, a cascade to the next rider, and the hand claim as the fallback. Words: dispatch, offer, online, offline, candidate; never driver, trip, job, match. Two lanes in this area; core-P5-L2 (store location) and admin-P5-L2 (form and board line) are in PLAN.md's Phase 5 dispatch table.

### rider-P5-L2 `rider-dispatch` (seeds `dispatch`; MODIFIED `rider-service`, `deliveries`)
- Migration `0005_dispatch.sql`: `riders.is_online boolean not null default false`, `riders.online_since timestamptz null`; `offers` (`id uuid`, `order_id uuid`, `order_number text`, `store_id uuid`, `rider_id uuid references riders`, `status offer_status` enum `offered | accepted | declined | expired`, `eta_seconds integer not null check (eta_seconds >= 0)`, `item_count`, `total_cents`, `payment_method`, `payment_status text default ''`, `pincode`, `placed_at`, `offered_at`, `expires_at`, `answered_at null`, timestamps); partial unique indexes `offers_open_order on offers (order_id) where status = 'offered'` and `offers_open_rider on offers (rider_id) where status = 'offered'`; index `(order_id, rider_id, answered_at)` for the 2-minute exclusion. Snapshots copied from core's order like `deliveries` so the offer renders without core.
- Config (fail fast, all optional with defaults): `SHOPPERSTOP_RIDER_DISPATCH_INTERVAL` (`3s`; `0` disables the loop, the tests and `make e2e` keep 3 s), `SHOPPERSTOP_RIDER_DISPATCH_OFFER_TTL` (`30s`), `SHOPPERSTOP_RIDER_DISPATCH_LOCATION_TTL` (`2m`), `SHOPPERSTOP_RIDER_DISPATCH_SPEED_KMH` (`20`). Durations parse with `time.ParseDuration`; a bad value is named at start like the other variables.
- `PUT /me/availability {online: bool}` → 200 `Rider` (now with `isOnline`); going online sets `online_since` when it was offline; going offline (or `POST /auth/sign-out`, or staff deactivation) sets both off and marks the rider's open offer `expired` so the next tick re-offers it. `GET /me` and the staff rider reads carry `isOnline`.
- `internal/dispatch`: `Tick(ctx, now)` does, in one transaction with `pg_try_advisory_xact_lock(<constant>)` (skip the tick when another process holds it): (1) expire every `offered` row past `expires_at` (log `offer expired`); (2) for each store that has at least one online rider: read core `StoreOrders(store, "ready")`, drop orders with a delivery or an open offer, oldest `placedAt` first; read the store from core (`Store(id)`, 30 s cache) and skip the store, logging once per minute, when it has no `location`; candidates = the store's online riders with no `assigned` delivery, no open offer, a `rider_locations` row younger than the location TTL, and no `declined` or `expired` offer on that order answered in the last 2 minutes; ETA = haversine metres from the position to the store (stdlib `math`; within 100 m is 0) divided by the speed; sort by `eta/60` floored, then the longer idle (now minus the later of `online_since` and the rider's last `delivered_at`), then rider id; insert the offer for the first candidate and remove that rider from the pool for this tick (log `offer made` with `offerId`, `orderId`, `number`, `riderId`, `etaSeconds`). Orders left without a candidate are untouched. The loop in `cmd/rider` runs `Tick` every interval with a real clock; tests call `Tick` with their own `now` and never sleep.
- `GET /me/offer` → 200 `Offer` `{id, orderId, number, placedAt, itemCount, totalCents, paymentMethod, paymentStatus?, pincode, etaSeconds, offeredAt, expiresAt}` for the signed-in rider's open offer, 204 without one (an expired one still unexpired by the tick counts as none: check `expires_at` in the read). `POST /me/offers/{id}/accept` → 201 `Delivery`: the offer must be the rider's and `offered` and unexpired (else 404 or 409 `conflict`); runs the existing claim transaction (`delivery.claim`) and marks the offer `accepted` in it; when core refuses (the order left `ready`) the offer becomes `expired` and the answer is 409; log `offer accepted`. `POST /me/offers/{id}/decline` → 204, marks `declined` (log `offer declined`); a second answer is 409.
- `GET /admin/orders/{orderId}/dispatch` (staff through core, store staff only for their store's order: read the order from core with the service token first) → `{online: <online riders at the store>, dispatch: on | off, offers: [{id, riderName, status, etaSeconds, offeredAt, answeredAt?}]}` newest first, at most 10; `dispatch` is `off` when the store has no location or the loop is disabled.
- `openapi.json`: tag `dispatch` for the new routes, `availability` under `rider-service`'s tag, examples everywhere; `make gen postman` committed (D19, D23); Postman `offerId` = `00000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000902`, the seeded `expired` offer of rider `...0801` on order `SS-DEMOAAAB` (seed without core, tied by the seed summary test).
- Tests (real database, fake core): availability on and off and its effect on the open offer; a tick with two online riders at different distances offers to the nearer; equal minute, longer idle wins; stale position excluded; a rider on a delivery excluded; decline then re-offer to the next rider in the next tick; expiry then re-offer; the 2-minute exclusion; no candidate leaves the order on the ready list and a hand claim still works; accept is exclusive with a hand claim (five concurrent, one 201); accept after the order left ready voids the offer; two concurrent ticks make one offer (advisory lock); store without location makes no offer; the admin read's store isolation; the contract test. Gates: `make test-rider`, `make gen postman` clean, strict validation.
